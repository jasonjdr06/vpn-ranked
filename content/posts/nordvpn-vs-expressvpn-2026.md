## Protocols

NordLynx is NordVPN's custom protocol combining WireGuard's speed with OpenVPN's proven security. It's excellent for streaming and speed.

**ExpressVPN: Lightway**

Lightway is ExpressVPN's proprietary protocol optimized for consistency and speed. It's newer but well-audited.

Both are excellent. NordLynx has longer track record; Lightway is newer but faster.

**Winner: NordVPN** for proven reliability, ExpressVPN for innovation.
