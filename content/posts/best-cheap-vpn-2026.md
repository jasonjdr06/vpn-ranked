## What You Sacrifice at Budget Prices

Budget VPNs typically trade off:
- **Speed**: Overloaded servers mean slower connections (but still usable for browsing/video)
- **Streaming success**: Works on main platforms but less reliable than premium options
- **Server selection**: Fewer locations means fewer choices
- **Customer support**: Less responsive technical support
- **App quality**: More basic interfaces and fewer configuration options

The privacy and encryption you get is generally just as strong as premium services.
