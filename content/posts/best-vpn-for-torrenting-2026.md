## Legal Considerations

Using a VPN for torrenting is legal in most countries. The VPN itself protects your identity, but:

- Your ISP cannot see what you're downloading (VPN masks it)
- Copyright holders cannot easily identify your IP address
- No-logs VPNs mean authorities cannot force disclosure of your activity

However, torrenting copyrighted content without authorization remains illegal regardless of VPN usage. VPNs are tools for privacy — they don't make copyright infringement legal.
