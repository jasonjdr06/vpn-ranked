## What to Look For in a VPN

**No-logs policy:** Should be independently audited, not just claimed. NordVPN, ExpressVPN, and Proton VPN have all been audited.

**Speed:** WireGuard-based protocols (NordLynx, Lightway, WireGuard) are dramatically faster than older OpenVPN implementations.

**Server network:** More servers = less congestion. Aim for 3,000+ with good coverage in your target regions.

**Streaming:** Not all VPNs reliably unblock Netflix, Disney+, BBC iPlayer. NordVPN and ExpressVPN are the most consistent.
