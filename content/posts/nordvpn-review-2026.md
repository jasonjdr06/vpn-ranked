## Speed Test Results: How Fast Is NordVPN?

Speed is the metric that matters most for day-to-day VPN use. Nobody wants a VPN that turns their fibre connection into dial-up. We tested NordVPN from three locations using a 500 Mbps base connection.

| Server Location | Download Speed | Upload Speed | Ping |
|----------------|---------------|-------------|------|
| No VPN (baseline) | 487 Mbps | 245 Mbps | 8 ms |
| UK (London) | 412 Mbps | 198 Mbps | 34 ms |
| US (New York) | 378 Mbps | 167 Mbps | 112 ms |
| Japan (Tokyo) | 301 Mbps | 142 Mbps | 178 ms |
| Nearby (same country) | 451 Mbps | 231 Mbps | 12 ms |

The results are impressive. NordVPN retained over 80% of our base speed on nearby servers and over 75% on transatlantic connections. The NordLynx protocol (based on WireGuard) is the reason for these strong numbers — it's significantly faster than the OpenVPN protocol that many VPNs still default to.

For comparison, most VPNs we've tested lose 30–40% of speed on nearby servers. NordVPN's speed performance puts it firmly in the top tier.

## Streaming: Does NordVPN Unblock Netflix and More?

One of the most common reasons people buy a VPN is to access geo-restricted streaming content. NordVPN has historically been one of the most reliable VPNs for this purpose, and in our 2026 testing, that reputation holds up.

We successfully unblocked Netflix US and UK libraries, BBC iPlayer, Disney+, Amazon Prime Video (US), and Hulu. The connection was stable enough for 4K streaming without buffering on all platforms except Hulu, which occasionally dropped to HD.

NordVPN's SmartPlay feature handles DNS routing automatically, which means you don't need to manually configure anything for streaming — just connect to a server in the right country and start watching.

The one streaming service we couldn't reliably access was DAZN, which has become increasingly aggressive with VPN detection. If DAZN is your primary reason for getting a VPN, you may need to test a few providers.

## Security and Privacy: Can You Trust NordVPN?

NordVPN is based in Panama, which sits outside the 5/9/14 Eyes surveillance alliances. This is a meaningful privacy advantage — Panama has no mandatory data retention laws, and NordVPN isn't legally required to hand over user data to foreign governments.

The company operates under a strict no-logs policy that has been independently audited three times by PricewaterhouseCoopers (PwC). The most recent audit in late 2025 confirmed that NordVPN does not store connection timestamps, session information, bandwidth usage, traffic data, or IP addresses.

**Threat Protection** is NordVPN's built-in security suite that blocks malicious websites, trackers, and ads at the DNS level. It works even when you're not connected to a VPN server, making it a useful always-on security layer.

**Double VPN** routes your traffic through two servers for an extra layer of encryption. This adds latency, so it's not ideal for everyday use, but it's valuable for journalists, activists, or anyone operating in high-risk environments.

**Kill Switch** automatically cuts your internet connection if the VPN drops unexpectedly, preventing your real IP from being exposed. Both the app-level and system-level kill switches worked reliably in our testing.

## NordVPN Apps and Usability

NordVPN offers apps for Windows, macOS, Linux, iOS, Android, Fire TV, and browser extensions for Chrome and Firefox. The desktop apps are clean and intuitive — connect with one click, or browse the server map to choose a specific location.

The mobile apps mirror the desktop experience well. Quick Connect finds the fastest nearby server, and you can save favourite servers for easy access. The iOS app includes Siri shortcuts, and the Android app supports split tunnelling (choosing which apps use the VPN).

One area where NordVPN has improved significantly is the Linux app, which now includes a full GUI rather than the command-line-only interface it had until recently.

You can connect up to 10 devices simultaneously on a single account, which is generous enough for a household with multiple phones, laptops, and a streaming device.

## NordVPN for Specific Use Cases

**For remote work:** NordVPN is excellent for securing public Wi-Fi connections and accessing company resources from abroad. The Meshnet feature lets you create a private network between your devices, which is useful for remote access without a corporate VPN.

**For gaming:** The dedicated gaming servers and NordLynx protocol minimise latency. We measured an average ping increase of only 4ms on nearby servers, which is negligible for most games. However, NordVPN won't help you reduce ping to servers that are geographically far from you.

**For the UAE and restricted regions:** NordVPN works in the UAE and other countries with VPN restrictions, though you may need to use obfuscated servers during periods of heavy censorship. The service consistently ranks among the most reliable VPNs for use in restrictive regions.

**For torrenting:** NordVPN allows P2P traffic on specialised servers and doesn't throttle torrent speeds. The no-logs policy means your download activity isn't recorded.
